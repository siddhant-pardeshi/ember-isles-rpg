# How to play Ember Isles

## Objective

Three quests, at your own pace, one save. Light the big lamp (Lantern Oil), relight the harbor beacons (Signal Embers), and — if you feel brave — bring the Ember Heart out of the cave. There is no score, no timer and no death: the isle is a place, not a test.

## Getting started

You wake on the village road. **Maren the lamplighter** stands by her cottage with a mark over her head — walk up and press **E** (or the ACT button). Dialogue advances with E or a tap. The **Quest** chip (top right) always tells you what the isle wants next, and **Momo**, the guide sprite, offers hints if you idle.

## The three quests

### 1. Lantern Oil (Maren)
Three **sunfruit** grow in the Whisper Woods, east past the tall grass. They glow amber and bob gently. Collect all three, return to Maren, and receive the **Lantern** — which opens the third quest and lights your way underground.

### 2. Signal Embers (Selu, at the harbor)
Selu will tell you the order: **dock, then well, then north path**. Light the braziers in exactly that order — the wrong brazier sulkily extinguishes the whole set and you start over. All three burning brings the ferries back and earns the Keeper's Charm.

### 3. The Cave Heart (unlocks after quest 1)
The cave mouth is north-east. Inside, your **lantern** holds a circle of warmth — the rest is dark. Slimes wander the tunnels: they bounce you back gently and dim the lantern for a breath, nothing worse. The **Ember Heart** rests in the far chamber. Bring it home and the whole isle lights up for you.

## The isle

- **Emberwick Village** — cottages, the well, Maren, Tama (counting fireflies).
- **Harbor Point** — the dock, Old Finn, Selu, the three braziers.
- **Whisper Woods** — tall grass, fireflies, sunfruit, data-slates.
- **Ember Cave** — the dark, the slimes, the Heart.

The **WHERE** chip (top left) names the region you're in as you cross each border.

## Data-slates

Six slates glow across the isle (three in the woods, one by the harbor, two around the village — and keep an eye on the cave). Reading one pauses the world for a short concept card about working with AI assistants. Correct answers are remembered in the Concepts codex; wrong ones get a one-line explanation and no penalty.

## Saving

Progress saves automatically after every dialogue, pickup and beacon: quest states, items, your position, everything. Close the tab, come back tomorrow — the isle continues. A full reset lives in Settings.

## Tips

1. Fireflies cluster over the sunfruit clearings — follow the lights.
2. The well is in the middle of the village square; one brazier waits beside it.
3. In the cave, hug walls: slimes bounce you toward open ground.
4. Talk to everyone twice — villagers say different things as your quests progress.
